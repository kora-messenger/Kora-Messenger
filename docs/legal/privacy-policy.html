Privacy Policy for Kora Messenger

Last Updated: August 31, 2026

Kora Messenger ("Kora," "our," "we," or "us") provides end-to-end encrypted messaging, voice notes, and real-time call translation services. We are deeply committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains our data practices, emphasizing that your personal conversations never leave your device.

1. Information We Do Not Collect (Your Privacy Shield)

Because Kora Messenger utilizes End-to-End Encryption (E2EE) and On-Device Artificial Intelligence, our servers are completely blind to your conversations.

  • Messages and Calls: We cannot read your text messages, listen to your calls, or view your shared media. Your data is encrypted before it leaves your phone and can only be decrypted by the intended recipient.
  • Translations: All text translation, voice note transcription, and live call language processing happen entirely on your local device hardware. Your decrypted texts and voice recordings are never sent to Kora's servers or any third-party cloud APIs for translation.

2. Information We Do Collect (To Make Kora Work)

To provide and manage our service, we collect a bare minimum amount of account data:

  • Account Credentials: To create an account, you must provide your email address, full name, username, and password. Registration is entirely email-based.
  • Optional Data: You may choose to add a phone number to your profile configuration, but this is entirely optional and does not require code verification.
  • Voice Vector Matrix: To allow your contacts to hear translations spoken in your voice style, a one-time mathematical model (a "Voice Vector") is generated on your phone. This file contains only math parameters (pitch, tone) and contains no actual spoken words. This encrypted file is stored on our server so your contacts can download it locally.
  • Customer Support: If you contact us for technical help, we receive the information you choose to send us (like bug reports or device models).

3. How Data is Processed (Our Hybrid Architecture)

  • On-Device Storage: Your messages are stored locally on your own mobile device. We do not keep logs of your messages on our servers once they are successfully delivered.
  • Zero Data Retention (ZDR) for Voice Notes: When a contact requests to hear your voice note translated into your voice style, the translation text and your mathematical Voice Vector are temporarily synthesized in secure, volatile server memory (RAM). This data is instantly wiped the second the audio file is generated. It is never written to a disk, never saved, and never used for training AI models.

4. Data Sharing and Third Parties

  • No Selling of Data: We do not sell, rent, or monetize your personal data. We do not share your private information with advertisers.
  • No Cloud Text Leaks: Unlike other apps, Kora does not route your conversations through third-party text APIs. Your written words remain on your hardware.

5. Your Rights and Data Control

You are in complete control of your data:

  • Managing Permissions: You can grant or revoke access to your microphone, contacts, and storage at any time via your device's system settings.
  • Account Deletion: You can choose to delete your Kora account at any time through the in-app settings. Upon deletion, your email registration, profile parameters, and public Voice Vector Matrix are immediately and permanently erased from our servers.

6. Premium Subscription & Privacy

Kora offers an optional Premium subscription for additional features such as custom themes, app icon switcher, and HD media quality. Your privacy protections (E2EE, on-device translation, zero data selling) apply equally to both free and Premium users. Premium status does not grant us any additional access to your private data, and we do not collect payment card information — all payments are processed by our payment provider (Paystack), and we only receive a subscription status flag.

7. Updates to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. If we make significant changes, we will notify you directly through the Kora Messenger app so you can review the updates.
