Acceptable Use Policy

What you can and cannot do on Kora Messenger. We keep Kora safe for everyone.

Last updated: September 1, 2026

Our Commitment

Kora Messenger is a platform for genuine, private communication. We're committed to keeping Kora safe, respectful, and free from abuse. This policy outlines what is and isn't acceptable on our platform.

Good news: 99% of our users will never need to read this. If you use Kora to chat with friends, share media, and communicate honestly — you're already following the rules.

What's Allowed

  • Private messaging — Chat with friends, family, and colleagues freely
  • Group conversations — Create groups and communities for any legitimate purpose
  • Voice & video calls — Up to 32 participants, for personal or business use
  • Media sharing — Photos, videos, documents, voice notes with contacts
  • AI features — Use Kora AI for translations, web search, and assistance
  • Business profiles — Use Kora for legitimate business communication
  • Communities & channels — Build communities around shared interests

What's Prohibited

  • Spam & bulk messaging — Sending unsolicited messages to people who didn't consent
  • Fraud & scams — Phishing, impersonation, financial scams, or deceptive practices
  • Claiming to be Kora — Any user who claims to be Kora Messenger or represents Kora without the official purple verified badge is committing fraud
  • Hate speech — Content that attacks people based on race, religion, ethnicity, gender, sexual orientation, disability, or nationality
  • Threats & harassment — Intimidating, stalking, or repeatedly contacting someone who asked you to stop
  • Violence & incitement — Content that promotes, organizes, or threatens violence
  • Child safety violations — Any content exploiting, endangering, or sexualizing minors (zero-tolerance, reported to authorities)
  • Terrorism & extremism — Content promoting terrorist organizations or violent extremism
  • Illegal goods & services — Selling or facilitating illegal drugs, weapons, or regulated goods
  • Non-consensual intimate content — Sharing intimate images without explicit consent
  • Malware & harmful code — Distributing viruses, spyware, or malicious software
  • Credential harvesting — Collecting passwords, tokens, or personal data through deception
  • Automated abuse — Using bots, scripts, or scrapers to access Kora at scale
  • Circumvention — Bypassing account restrictions or security measures

Zero-tolerance violations (immediate ban + law enforcement): Child exploitation · Terrorism · Non-consensual intimate content · Credible threats of violence

Reporting Violations

  • In-app reporting — Tap and hold a message → Report
  • Report a user — Profile → Report User
  • Email — abuse@koramessenger.com

We review all reports within 48 hours and take appropriate action, which may include warnings, temporary suspensions, or permanent bans.

Enforcement Actions

  • Warning — First minor violation; educational notice
  • Temporary suspension — Repeated or moderate violations (7-30 days)
  • Permanent ban — Severe or repeated violations
  • Law enforcement referral — For illegal activity or child safety violations

Suspended users can appeal through the app (Settings → Account → Appeal Suspension) or by emailing appeals@koramessenger.com.

Contact

Report abuse: abuse@koramessenger.com
Appeals: appeals@koramessenger.com
