Refund Eligibility

We believe in fair, transparent refunds. Here's when you're eligible:

SituationEligible?Window
New monthly subscriptionYesWithin 7 days, if premium features unused
New annual subscriptionYesWithin 7 days, if premium features unused
Monthly renewal (auto-renew)YesWithin 7 days of renewal, if unused
Annual renewal (auto-renew)YesWithin 7 days of renewal, if unused
Accidental purchaseYesWithin 7 days, case-by-case
Service outage during subscriptionYesCase-by-case, prorated

Refund Timeframes

  • 7-day window — Full refund if you haven't used premium features after subscribing or renewing
  • Used premium features — If you've used premium features (e.g., applied a custom app icon, sent HD media), we evaluate case-by-case but may offer partial refund
  • After 7 days — Contact us with your reason. We handle these fairly and individually
  • Annual subscriptions — If you cancel mid-year, we may offer a prorated refund for unused months at our discretion

How we check "unused": We check whether you've accessed any premium-only feature (app icon switching, HD media, premium stickers, etc.). Simply having premium active doesn't count as "used" — you need to have actually used a premium feature.

How to Request a Refund

  • In-app: Settings → Premium → Request Refund
  • Email: billing@koramessenger.com with your account email and reason
  • Include: Your Kora ID, email, approximate purchase date, and reason for refund
  • Response time: We respond to all refund requests within 48 hours

Processing Time

  • Approval: Within 48 hours of your request
  • Paystack processing: 1-3 business days
  • Your bank: 5-10 business days to appear on your statement
  • Total time: Typically 7-14 business days from request to money in your account

Refunds are processed back to the original payment method. We cannot refund to a different card or account.

Exceptions & Special Cases

  • Service outage — If Kora Premium was unavailable for an extended period, we offer prorated refunds or subscription extensions
  • Billing error — If you were charged incorrectly, we refund the full erroneous charge immediately
  • Double charge — If Paystack charged you twice for the same subscription, we refund the duplicate immediately

Non-Refundable Cases

  • Subscriptions where premium features were actively used beyond the 7-day window
  • Repeated subscribe-refund-subscribe patterns (we detect and block this)
  • Refunds requested after the billing period has fully elapsed (30+ days for monthly, 12+ months for annual)
  • Accounts suspended or terminated for Terms of Service violations — no refunds for suspended accounts, including any remaining premium balance
  • Charges from more than 60 days ago (beyond Paystack's dispute window)

Even in non-refundable cases, we encourage you to contact us. We handle every case individually and may make exceptions for genuine circumstances.
