Overview

Kora Messenger Premium is an optional subscription that unlocks enhanced customization and AI features. Premium is not required to use Kora — all core messaging, calling, and AI assistant features are free for everyone.

Kora AI Assistant is free for all users. We do not gate AI chat, web search, or image generation behind a paywall. Premium unlocks AI Voice Translation and customization enhancements.

The following features are exclusive to Kora Premium subscribers:

Free Features (No Premium Required)

  • Messaging — All text, voice, and media messaging is free
  • Voice & Video Calls — 1-on-1 and group calls (up to 32) are free
  • Kora AI Assistant — Web search, translations, image generation — all free
  • End-to-End Encryption — Full E2EE for all users, no paywall
  • Communities & Channels — Create and join communities for free
  • Groups — Create and participate in group chats for free
  • Status & Stories — Post and view status updates for free
  • Biometric Lock — App lock and chat vaults for free

Our promise: We will never put core messaging, calling, encryption, or AI behind a paywall. Premium is for customization only.

Billing & Payments

  • Payments are processed by Paystack — a PCI-DSS compliant payment processor
  • We never see or store your card information — Paystack handles all payment data
  • Subscriptions auto-renew at the end of each billing cycle unless cancelled
  • You'll receive an email receipt for each payment
  • Your premium status activates immediately upon successful payment

Cancellation

  • Cancel anytime in Settings → Premium → Cancel Subscription
  • Your premium features remain active until the end of your current billing period
  • No cancellation fees or penalties
  • Cancelling does not delete your account — you keep all messages and data
  • After cancellation, your account reverts to the free tier automatically

Refunds

  • Within 7 days of a new subscription: full refund available if you haven't used premium features
  • Within 7 days of an annual renewal: full refund available if you cancel before using premium features
  • After 7 days: refunds are evaluated case-by-case. Email support@koramessenger.com with your reason
  • Refunds are processed back to your original payment method via Paystack
  • Refund processing time depends on your bank (typically 5-10 business days)

Fair use: We don't refund if you've repeatedly subscribed and refunded to access premium features for free.

Verified Kora Accounts

Official Kora accounts carry a purple verified badge. This badge confirms the account is genuinely operated by Kora Messenger or Nexora Technologies. Any user claiming to be Kora or to represent Kora without this purple verified badge is fraudulent and should be reported immediately.

  • What the badge looks like: A purple checkmark next to the account name — only visible on genuine Kora-operated accounts
  • Who gets verified: Only official Kora Messenger and Nexora Technologies accounts. No other users can purchase or request this badge.
  • How to verify: Tap the account name in any chat. If it's a genuine Kora account, the purple verified badge appears in the profile header.
  • Reporting fraud: If anyone claims to be Kora without the purple badge, report them via the in-app report function or email security@koramessenger.com
  • Kora will never: Ask for your password, PIN, or payment via chat. Kora will never message you asking to verify your account by clicking a link.

Price Changes

  • We may change premium pricing with at least 30 days notice
  • Existing subscribers keep their current price until the next renewal
  • You'll be notified in-app and via email before any price increase
  • If you disagree with a price change, you can cancel before the new price takes effect
