Community Guidelines

How to be a great member of the Kora community. Simple, human, and fair.

Last updated: September 1, 2026

Our Community Values

Kora Messenger is more than an app — it's a community of people connecting with friends, family, and colleagues. These guidelines help keep Kora a safe, respectful, and enjoyable place for everyone.

The golden rule: Treat others the way you want to be treated. If you wouldn't want it sent to you, don't send it to someone else.

Do: Be a Good Community Member

  • Be authentic — Use your real identity. Connect with people you know and trust.
  • Be respectful — Disagree without being disagreeable. People have different opinions, and that's okay.
  • Be kind — A little kindness goes a long way. Compliment, encourage, and support others.
  • Protect privacy — Don't share others' personal information without consent. Don't forward private conversations.
  • Report problems — If you see something wrong, report it. You help keep the community safe.
  • Use communities responsibly — Keep discussions on-topic in communities and channels. Respect community-specific rules set by admins.
  • Respect boundaries — If someone asks you to stop messaging them, respect their wishes.
  • Credit creators — When sharing content from others, give credit where it's due.

Don't: Violations That Get You Removed

  • Don't spam — Unsolicited bulk messages, chain letters, and "forward to 10 people" scams
  • Don't harass — Repeated unwanted contact, threats, stalking, or targeted attacks
  • Don't spread hate — Content attacking people based on race, religion, gender, sexual orientation, disability, or nationality
  • Don't share illegal content — Content that violates applicable laws in your jurisdiction or ours
  • Don't exploit minors — Zero tolerance. Immediate ban and law enforcement report
  • Don't spread misinformation deliberately — Especially content that could cause real-world harm
  • Don't impersonate — Pretending to be someone else to deceive or harm
  • Don't claim to be Kora — Only official accounts with the purple verified badge can represent Kora Messenger. Anyone else claiming to be Kora is committing fraud
  • Don't share non-consensual intimate content — Sharing intimate images without explicit, informed consent
  • Don't organize violence — Content promoting, organizing, or threatening violence
  • Don't sell illegal goods — Drugs, weapons, or any illegal regulated goods and services

Zero-tolerance violations result in immediate permanent ban + law enforcement: Child exploitation · Terrorism · Non-consensual intimate content · Credible threats of violence

How to Report Violations

  • Report a message: Tap and hold the message → Report
  • Report a user: Go to their profile → Report User
  • Report a community/channel: Community info → Report
  • Email: abuse@koramessenger.com (include screenshots if possible)

Every report is reviewed within 48 hours. You'll receive a notification when action is taken. Reports are confidential — the reported person never knows who reported them.

Enforcement Actions

  • Warning — First minor violation. Educational notice explaining what was wrong.
  • Temporary mute — Can read but not send messages for a period (1 hour to 24 hours).
  • Temporary suspension — Account access restricted for 7-30 days. Can appeal.
  • Permanent ban — Account permanently deactivated. Data deleted within 30 days.
  • Law enforcement referral — For criminal activity, child safety, or credible threats.

Severity and history determine the action. We don't automatically ban for first minor mistakes — we educate. But repeated or severe violations escalate quickly.

Appeals Process

  • In-app: Settings → Account → Appeal Suspension
  • Email: appeals@koramessenger.com
  • Timeline: Appeals reviewed within 72 hours
  • Fair review: A different person reviews each appeal — not the one who made the original decision
  • What to include: Your Kora ID, why you believe the action was wrong, and any context we should know

We make mistakes sometimes. If you believe you were wrongly suspended or banned, please appeal. We'd rather reinstate a wrongfully banned user than leave them locked out.

Community Leaders

  • Group admins — Set group rules, remove members, and manage group settings
  • Community admins — Manage communities, add/remove groups, set community-wide rules
  • Channel admins — Post announcements, manage subscribers, control channel settings

Community leaders are responsible for enforcing their own community rules in addition to these guidelines. If a community leader violates these guidelines, they may lose leadership privileges even if their community itself remains.
