What is Kora AI

Kora AI is an AI assistant built into Kora Messenger. It's available in every chat and helps with web searches, image generation, and general questions. Kora AI is free for all users — no premium subscription required. AI Voice Translation (real-time voice-to-text translation) is a premium feature.

Free forever. We will never put Kora AI chat, web search, or image generation behind a paywall. AI Voice Translation requires Kora Premium.

Data Handling

  • Voice data — Processed on your device. Your voice never leaves your phone for translation or synthesis.
  • Chat messages — When you ask Kora AI a question, only your specific query is sent for processing. Your other messages are not shared.
  • No training on your data — We do not use your conversations, voice, or AI queries to train AI models.
  • No data retention — AI queries sent to the cloud are processed in real-time and not stored after generating a response.
  • Aggregated analytics — We only collect anonymous, aggregated usage statistics (e.g., "voice translation was used X times"). No content.

Your conversations stay private. Kora AI processes your specific query in isolation. It does not read your other messages, contacts, or media to generate responses.

On-Device Processing

Kora Messenger prioritizes on-device AI processing to protect your privacy:

  • Speech-to-text (STT) — Uses Google ML Kit (Android) and CoreML (iOS) running locally on your device
  • Translation — On-device ML models handle translation without sending text to the cloud
  • Voice synthesis (TTS) — Mathematical voice vectors generate speech locally
  • Biometric processing — Fingerprint and Face ID data never leave the secure enclave/keystore

E2EE privacy preserved: Because voice translation and synthesis happen on-device, your plaintext never touches a server. End-to-end encryption is maintained end-to-end.

AI Guardrails

Kora AI operates under strict guardrails to protect users and maintain trust:

  • No internal disclosures — Kora AI will never reveal internal implementation details, specific translation providers, or owner-specific account policies
  • No harmful content — Kora AI refuses to generate content that promotes violence, hate speech, self-harm, or illegal activity
  • No impersonation — Kora AI will not pretend to be a real person or create content on behalf of real individuals
  • No code execution — Kora AI cannot execute code, access your files, or perform actions on your device
  • No data extraction — Kora AI will not attempt to extract personal data, passwords, or sensitive information from users
  • Age-appropriate — Kora AI is designed to be appropriate for users aged 13+ and will not generate adult content
  • Transparency — AI-generated content is always labeled as such within the chat

Limitations

AI can make mistakes. Kora AI responses are generated and may contain inaccuracies. Always verify critical information (medical, legal, financial) independently.

  • Kora AI may not have up-to-date information on very recent events unless using the web search feature
  • Voice translation accuracy varies by language, accent, and audio quality
  • AI image generation may produce unexpected or imperfect results
  • Kora AI cannot access your private messages, contacts, or media to inform its responses
  • Kora AI responses do not represent the official position of Nexora Technologies

You are responsible for how you use AI-generated content. Kora AI is a tool — not a source of professional advice.

Voice Translation Specifics Premium

  • Input source — Uses VOICE_COMMUNICATION source (microphone only, isolated from system audio and notifications)
  • Processing — On-device via ML Kit (Android) / CoreML (iOS). No cloud APIs for plaintext.
  • Supported languages — 100+ languages including Yoruba, Igbo, Hausa, Swahili, French, Arabic, and more
  • Architecture — Zero-cost strategy using on-device STT + free-tier LLM calls for complex translations only
  • Privacy — Voice data is processed and discarded locally. Never uploaded, never stored on servers.

AI Image Generation

  • Generate images from text descriptions directly in chat
  • Images are watermarked with a small Kora AI indicator
  • You own the images you generate and can share them freely
  • Nexora Technologies retains a license to the underlying generation model, not your prompts or generated images
  • Prohibited: Generating images of real people without consent, explicit content, or content that violates our Acceptable Use Policy

AI Web Search

  • Kora AI can search the web for real-time information when you ask
  • Search queries are sent to our backend API, which queries search providers and returns results
  • Your search query is processed in real-time and not stored after the response is generated
  • Web search results include source links for transparency
  • Kora AI clearly labels which parts of a response come from web search vs. its own knowledge

Your Control

  • Disable AI — You can disable Kora AI in Settings → AI → Disable AI Assistant
  • Disable voice translation — Turn off in Settings → AI → Voice Translation (Premium feature)
  • Disable web search — Turn off in Settings → AI → Web Search
  • Disable image generation — Turn off in Settings → AI → Image Generation
  • Delete AI history — Clear all AI conversations in Settings → AI → Clear AI History

Disabling AI features does not affect your messaging, calling, or encryption. It only turns off AI-specific capabilities.

Transparency Commitment

We commit to: Clearly labeling AI-generated content · Never training on your private data · Processing on-device wherever possible · Disclosing what goes to the cloud · Giving you full control to disable any AI feature
