Overview

Kora Messenger is a privacy-first messaging application developed by Nexora Technologies. We designed Kora with a simple principle: your conversations are yours.

We do not sell your data. We do not show ads. We do not share your personal information with third parties for marketing purposes. Your messages and calls are end-to-end encrypted — not even we can read them.

We collect only the minimum data needed to make Kora work. Here's exactly what we handle:

Your messages and calls are end-to-end encrypted. We store encrypted copies for delivery purposes, but we cannot decrypt or read them. Only the sender and recipient hold the decryption keys.

How We Use Your Data

We use your data exclusively to provide and improve Kora Messenger:

  • Account creation & authentication — verifying your phone number and email
  • Message delivery — routing encrypted messages between users
  • Contact discovery — finding friends already on Kora via hashed phone numbers
  • Call connections — establishing peer-to-peer encrypted calls
  • AI features — on-device processing for voice translation and AI assistant (data never leaves your device)
  • Security & abuse prevention — detecting spam, fraud, and Terms of Service violations
  • Push notifications — delivering message and call notifications via Firebase Cloud Messaging
  • App improvements — aggregated, anonymous crash reports and performance metrics

We never: Sell your data · Show ads · Share your info for marketing · Read your messages · Track your location for advertising

End-to-End Encryption

Every message, voice note, photo, video, document, and call in Kora Messenger is protected by end-to-end encryption (E2EE) using the Signal Protocol.

  • Messages — Encrypted on your device with a key only the recipient can use. We store encrypted copies for delivery but cannot decrypt them.
  • Calls — Peer-to-peer WebRTC connections with DTLS encryption. Call content never passes through our servers.
  • Media — Photos, videos, and documents are encrypted before upload and can only be decrypted by the intended recipient.
  • AI Voice Translation — All speech-to-text and translation processing happens on your device using ML Kit / CoreML. Your voice never leaves your phone.

Safety numbers — You can verify your encryption is working by comparing safety numbers with your chat partner. If they match, your conversation is completely private.

Third-Party Services

Kora uses minimal third-party services, each carefully chosen for privacy:

  • Firebase Cloud Messaging (Google) — Delivers push notifications. Google does not have access to message content.
  • Paystack — Processes premium subscription payments. Payment card data is handled by Paystack, never stored on our servers.
  • Google Play Services — App licensing and distribution on Google Play.
  • ML Kit / CoreML — On-device speech recognition and translation. All processing happens locally on your device.

We do not use any advertising SDKs, tracking pixels, or analytics services that profile users. Our crash reporting is anonymous and aggregated.

Your Rights

Under GDPR, CCPA, and NDPR (Nigeria), you have the following rights regarding your data:

  • Access — Request a copy of all data we hold about you
  • Correction — Update or correct inaccurate information
  • Deletion — Request permanent deletion of your account and data
  • Portability — Export your data in a machine-readable format
  • Objection — Object to certain data processing activities
  • Withdrawal — Withdraw consent for data processing at any time

To exercise any of these rights, email us at support@koramessenger.com. We respond within 30 days.

Data Retention

We retain your data only as long as needed to provide the service:

  • Account data — Kept while your account is active. Deleted within 30 days of account deletion.
  • Messages — Encrypted copies stored until delivered. Undelivered messages are deleted after 30 days.
  • Media — Encrypted media stored for 90 days after sending, then permanently deleted.
  • Call logs — Duration and timestamp kept for 90 days. Call content is never stored.
  • Crash reports — Aggregated and anonymized, retained for 12 months.

You can request early deletion of your data at any time by deleting your account or contacting us.

Children's Privacy

Kora Messenger is not intended for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has registered, please contact us and we will delete the account immediately.

Users aged 13-17 must have parental permission to use Kora Messenger.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we'll:

  • Update the "Last updated" date at the top of this page
  • Notify you through the app at least 7 days before changes take effect
  • Maintain a version history accessible at koramessenger.com/privacy-history

Continued use of Kora Messenger after changes take effect constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
