What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files stored on your device when you visit a website. They help the site remember your preferences, analyze traffic, and provide a better experience.

Kora's approach: We use the absolute minimum cookies necessary. No advertising cookies. No cross-site tracking. No selling cookie data.

Types of Cookies We Use

  • Essential — Required for the website to function (navigation, security). Cannot be disabled.
  • Preference — Remember your settings (dark mode, language). Optional.
  • Analytics — Anonymous, aggregated traffic data to improve the site. Optional.

We do NOT use: Advertising cookies · Cross-site tracking · Retargeting pixels · Social media tracking

Third-Party Cookies

Our website may use basic hosting services which may set essential server-side cookies. We do not embed social media widgets, advertising networks, or tracking pixels on our website.

The Kora Messenger app does not use cookies. It uses local, on-device storage (encrypted) for your preferences and cache.

Managing Cookies

You can control cookies through your browser settings:

  • Chrome: Settings → Privacy → Cookies
  • Safari: Preferences → Privacy → Cookies
  • Firefox: Settings → Privacy → Cookies
  • Edge: Settings → Cookies → Manage

Disabling all cookies may affect website functionality. Essential cookies cannot be disabled as they're required for the site to work.

Kora App & Local Storage

The Kora Messenger mobile app uses local on-device storage (not cookies) for:

  • Encrypted message cache (E2E encrypted)
  • User preferences (theme, language, notification settings)
  • On-device AI models for voice translation
  • Biometric authentication data (stored in secure enclave / keystore)

This data never leaves your device and is deleted when you uninstall the app or delete your account.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Cookie Policy when we change how we use cookies. We'll update the date at the top and notify you in the app if material changes are made.
