Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 19, 2026

Kora Messenger

Welcome to Kora Messenger ("we," "our," or "us"). We are committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our mobile application, Kora Messenger (the "App"), including our standard and premium subscription features.

Please read this Privacy Policy carefully. If you do not agree with the terms of this Privacy Policy, please do not access or use the App.

1. Information We Collect and How We Use It

A. End-to-End Encrypted Communications

Your privacy is our core priority. Kora Messenger provides end-to-end encryption for all text messages, media attachments, and call streams.

  • No Access to Content: This encryption means that your messages, photos, videos, and voice calls are encrypted on your device and can only be decrypted by the recipient.
  • Zero Visibility: We cannot read your messages or listen to your calls under any circumstances.

B. Premium Features Data Processing

To provide our Premium Subscription features, we process specific data locally on your device or safely through transient cloud processing:

  • Voice Note & Live Call Translation: When you use our voice note translation or live video/voice call translator, audio data is processed to generate text or audio translations. This data is processed strictly in real-time to execute the translation and is not permanently stored on our servers.
  • Voice Creation Feature (Audio/Video Upload): When you upload audio or video files to create a custom synthesized voice, we process the voice biometrics and audio data solely to generate your custom voice model. This data is used strictly for your voice generation feature and is not shared with third parties or used for any other purpose.

C. Device Permissions

To function correctly, Kora Messenger requires explicit access to certain device features. You can opt out or revoke these permissions at any time through your device settings:

  • Camera and Gallery Permissions: Allows you to take photos/videos directly within the app, share existing media with your contacts, and utilize the live video translator feature.
  • Audio / Microphone Permissions: Essential for recording voice notes, making end-to-end encrypted audio/video calls, and utilizing the voice creation and translation features.
  • Location Permissions: Enables you to share your real-time or precise location with your contacts in chats when you explicitly choose to do so. We do not track your location in the background or sell your location data.

2. Payments and Billing Information

Our premium tier subscriptions are handled entirely using native mobile commerce solutions.

  • Platform Processing: All premium transactions are safely processed directly through Apple App Store Billing or Google Play Store Billing.
  • No Financial Storage: Kora Messenger does not capture, store, or process your credit card numbers, banking details, or financial accounts on our servers. Your transactions are fully bound by the privacy policies and terms of service of Apple or Google respectively.

3. Data Retention and Security

  • Transient Processing: Data processed for premium features (such as translation audio) is kept only for the duration required to complete the action.
  • Security Measures: We use standard industry practices, along with end-to-end encryption protocols, to ensure your metadata and account access remain secure against unauthorized access, loss, or disclosure.

4. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page and updating the "Last Updated" date at the top of this document. You are advised to review this Privacy Policy periodically for any changes.
