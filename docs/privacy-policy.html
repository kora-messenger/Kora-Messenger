Kora Messenger

1. Introduction

Kora Messenger ("Kora," "we," "us," or "our") is a messaging application that allows users to create accounts, send and receive messages, and connect with others. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and the choices you have.

By creating an account or using Kora Messenger, you agree to the collection and use of your information as described in this policy. If you do not agree, please do not use the app.

2. Information We Collect

2.1 Account Information

When you create a Kora account, we collect:

  • Email address — used for account verification, login, and password recovery
  • Full name — displayed on your profile
  • Username — a unique handle other users can search for you by
  • Kora ID — a unique numeric identifier assigned to your account
  • Phone number — used for account verification and device management
  • Profile photo and bio — optional, displayed on your profile
  • Password — stored as a one-way hash; we never see your plain-text password

2.2 Message Content

Kora is a messaging app. When you send or receive messages, that content is transmitted through our servers to deliver it to the intended recipient. Message data includes:

  • Text messages
  • Timestamps of messages
  • Sender and recipient identifiers
  • Read receipts and delivery status

2.3 Device and Usage Information

We collect limited technical information to provide and improve our services:

  • Device model, operating system version, and app version
  • Trusted device records (used for multi-device session management)
  • Approximate login location (for security and fraud detection)
  • Crash reports and performance metrics (anonymized)

2.4 Verification Codes

When you verify your email or phone number, we generate a temporary verification code that is sent to you via email. These codes expire shortly after issuance and are deleted once used or expired.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use your information to:

  • Create and authenticate your account
  • Send and deliver messages between users
  • Verify your identity via email verification codes
  • Manage multi-device sessions and trusted devices
  • Send security alerts (e.g., new device login)
  • Send password reset emails when requested
  • Prevent abuse, spam, and unauthorized access
  • Provide customer support
  • Comply with legal obligations

4. How We Store Your Data

Currently, Kora Messenger's backend is hosted on Base44's managed infrastructure. All data is stored in encrypted databases with row-level security. Access to user data is restricted to authorized systems only.

Kora Messenger is designed with a domain-swappable architecture. In the future, user data will be migrated to a self-hosted backend on infrastructure controlled by the developer. When this migration occurs, this policy will be updated to reflect the new data storage arrangements.

5. Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal data. We share data only in these limited circumstances:

  • Email delivery services — We use an SMTP email provider to send verification codes and password reset emails. Only the email address and the content of the email being sent are shared.
  • Infrastructure providers — Our hosting provider (currently Base44) processes data on our behalf under a data processing agreement.
  • Legal compliance — If required by law, court order, or government regulation, we may disclose information as necessary.

6. Data Retention

  • Active accounts — Your data is retained while your account is active.
  • Deleted accounts — When you delete your account, we permanently remove your profile information, messages, and associated data within 30 days.
  • Verification codes — Deleted immediately after use or upon expiration.
  • Device records — Removed when you log out of a device or after 90 days of inactivity.

7. Your Rights

Depending on your jurisdiction, you may have the following rights regarding your personal data:

  • Access — Request a copy of your personal data
  • Rectification — Correct inaccurate or incomplete data
  • Erasure — Request deletion of your account and associated data
  • Portability — Receive your data in a structured, machine-readable format
  • Objection — Object to certain processing of your data
  • Withdrawal of consent — Withdraw consent at any time (where processing is based on consent)

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at KoraMessengerOfficial@gmail.com.

8. Security

We take reasonable measures to protect your data, including:

  • Passwords are stored as one-way cryptographic hashes (never in plain text)
  • All network communication uses HTTPS/TLS encryption
  • Row-level security on database records
  • Verification codes with expiration and attempt limits
  • Trusted device management to detect unauthorized access

However, no method of transmission or electronic storage is 100% secure. We cannot guarantee absolute security.

9. Children's Privacy

Kora Messenger is not intended for children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If we become aware that we have collected such data, we will delete it promptly. If you believe a child under 13 has created an account, please contact us.

10. Third-Party Links

Kora Messenger may contain links to third-party websites or services. We are not responsible for the privacy practices or content of these third parties. We encourage you to review their privacy policies.

11. Cookies and Tracking

Kora Messenger is a mobile application and does not use cookies. We do not use third-party tracking pixels or advertising SDKs. We may use anonymized crash analytics to improve app stability.

12. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will update the "Last updated" date at the top of this page. For significant changes, we may notify you via email or through the app. Your continued use of Kora Messenger after changes take effect constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
