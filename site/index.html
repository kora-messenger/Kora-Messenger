Features

Everything you need to stay connected

Kora brings together private messaging, calling, and AI — built for speed and privacy from the ground up.

End-to-end encrypted

Your messages and calls are secured so only you and the person you're talking to can read or hear them. Not even Kora can see them.

Kora AI, free for everyone

Writing help, chat summaries, instant answers, and live translation — Kora AI is built in and completely free for every user.

Available everywhere

Start a conversation on your phone and pick it right back up on the web — synced instantly across all your devices.

Voice-to-text translation

Voice messages are transcribed and translated on the fly, so you never miss what was said — in any language.

Groups, communities & channels

Organize conversations at any scale — from close friend groups to large communities and broadcast channels.

Fast, lightweight, reliable

Built to feel instant everywhere — no lag, no bloat, whether you're on 5G or a spotty connection.

Security

Privacy is not optional

Every message, call, photo, and voice note on Kora is protected by end-to-end encryption. Your conversations stay between you and the people you're talking to — no one else, not even Kora.

  • End-to-end encryption for all messages & calls
  • Keys generated & stored on your device
  • No ad tracking, no data selling, ever
  • Security codes & device verification
